# Production Prompts — VANTA / AFTER HOURS

These prompts document the production process used for the final VANTA campaign. Repetition is intentional where it reinforces traveler, product, wardrobe, and visual-world continuity.

Strong reference language such as “exact character reference” is preserved here because it functioned as a generation constraint. It should not be interpreted as a claim that the final generated images achieve pixel-perfect identity or exact duplication.

## Prompt 01 — Departure / Hero

```text
Create a cinematic premium travel advertising image for a fictional luggage brand called VANTA.

The hero product is the VANTA Carry-On 01, a premium compact hard-shell cabin suitcase with a matte graphite shell, subtle horizontal ribbing, rounded rectangular proportions, a black telescopic handle, four minimal spinner wheels, discreet brushed-metal hardware, and a small understated VANTA wordmark.

Feature a male traveler in his early thirties with short dark hair and an understated, refined appearance. He wears a charcoal wool coat over a black knit top, dark tailored trousers, clean black leather shoes, and a minimal black watch.

Create a cinematic nighttime departure scene inside or immediately outside a modern international transport terminal.

Show the traveler naturally moving through or pausing within the architecture while pulling or standing beside the VANTA carry-on. The luggage must remain clearly visible and commercially important without turning the scene into a static product catalog image.

Build a sophisticated nocturnal visual world using deep navy, petrol blue, charcoal, wet asphalt grey, cool steel, and restrained warm amber practical lights.

Use modern glass architecture, subtle reflections, polished or slightly wet surfaces, distant city or terminal lights, and realistic environmental depth.

Lighting should feel cinematic and photographic: cool ambient blue light balanced with small warm tungsten or amber practical sources. Use realistic reflections, soft atmospheric depth, controlled contrast, and subtle motion cues.

The image should feel like a premium international travel campaign — quiet, cinematic, metropolitan, and emotionally restrained.

Prioritize:
- realistic human anatomy
- believable walking or standing posture
- natural hand interaction with the suitcase
- realistic suitcase scale
- accurate wheel and telescopic-handle geometry
- believable hard-shell material
- cinematic environmental lighting
- premium commercial composition

Avoid fashion-runway posing, bright daylight, beige luxury interiors, excessive neon, cyberpunk styling, crowded terminals, dominant advertising signage, visible unrelated brand logos, warped luggage, broken wheels, distorted hands, text overlays, and watermarks.

Vertical cinematic advertising composition.
```

## Prompt 02 — In Transit / First Attempt

```text
Create a cinematic premium travel campaign image for VANTA using the provided hero image as the exact character, wardrobe, luggage, and visual-world reference.

Preserve the same male traveler identity, short dark hair, charcoal wool coat, black knit top, dark tailored trousers, clean black leather shoes, minimal black watch, and the same VANTA Carry-On 01 suitcase with matte graphite hard shell, subtle horizontal ribbing, black telescopic handle, four spinner wheels, and understated VANTA wordmark.

Do not redesign or reinterpret the traveler or the suitcase.

Create a second scene that clearly feels like the journey is in progress. Show the traveler actively moving through the transport environment — such as walking through a terminal corridor, crossing a covered drop-off zone, or moving along a modern transit walkway.

The suitcase should remain clearly visible and important, but the image should feel more dynamic and narrative-driven than the hero frame.

Keep the same nocturnal premium travel world: deep navy, petrol blue, charcoal, cool steel, wet pavement or reflective flooring, modern glass architecture, subtle airport or transit cues, distant lights, and restrained warm amber practical lighting.

Use cinematic lighting and environmental depth. Add subtle motion cues through stride, coat movement, wheel direction, reflections, or slight environmental blur, while keeping the image realistic and commercially polished.

Avoid fashion-runway posing, static standing compositions too similar to the hero frame, bright daylight, cluttered crowds, dominant signage, excessive neon, warped suitcase proportions, distorted hands, broken wheels, text overlays, and watermarks.

Vertical cinematic advertising composition.
```

## Prompt 02 v2 — In Transit / Final Direction

```text
Create a cinematic premium travel campaign image for VANTA using the provided hero image as the exact character, wardrobe, luggage, and visual-world reference.

Preserve the same male traveler identity, hairstyle, charcoal wool coat, black knit top, dark tailored trousers, black leather shoes, minimal black watch, and the same VANTA Carry-On 01 suitcase.

Preserve the recognizable suitcase design: matte graphite hard shell, subtle horizontal ribbing, rounded rectangular proportions, black telescopic handle, four minimal spinner wheels, discreet hardware, and understated VANTA branding.

Do not redesign the traveler or luggage.

IMPORTANT: create a composition that is clearly different from the hero image.

Show the traveler in SIDE PROFILE or three-quarter side view, actively walking from left to right through a long modern terminal corridor.

Do not show him walking directly toward the camera.

He should be mid-stride with a purposeful natural walking posture, looking forward in the direction of travel rather than looking toward the viewer.

The VANTA suitcase should roll slightly behind him at an angle, pulled naturally by one hand through the extended telescopic handle.

Use a wider environmental composition with more negative space and visible travel direction.

Place the camera approximately waist-height or slightly lower and positioned perpendicular to his movement, as if capturing a cinematic tracking shot.

Introduce subtle panning-style motion blur in the distant background and floor reflections while keeping the traveler and suitcase sharp and readable.

Use long architectural lines, glass walls, structural columns, overhead terminal lighting, distant gates, and subtle silhouettes of other travelers far in the background to create depth and progression.

Maintain the established VANTA nocturnal visual world:
- deep navy
- petrol blue
- charcoal
- cool steel
- reflective dark flooring
- restrained amber practical lights

The image should communicate that the journey is actively progressing rather than presenting another posed campaign portrait.

Prioritize:
- strong side-profile movement
- realistic walking anatomy
- believable coat movement
- natural suitcase wheel direction
- realistic telescopic-handle angle
- clear separation between traveler and luggage
- cinematic tracking-shot composition
- environmental depth
- continuity with the established VANTA campaign

Avoid:
- front-facing walking pose
- centered hero composition
- traveler looking toward camera
- suitcase positioned exactly beside the traveler
- static standing pose
- composition similar to the departure hero image
- fashion-runway posing
- excessive crowds
- excessive neon
- bright daylight
- warped luggage
- broken wheels
- distorted hands
- text overlays
- watermarks

Vertical cinematic advertising composition.
```

## Prompt 03 — Arrival / Detail Narrative

```text
Create a cinematic premium travel campaign image for VANTA using the provided hero image as the exact character, wardrobe, luggage, and visual-world reference.

Preserve the same male traveler identity, short dark hair, charcoal wool coat, black knit top, dark tailored trousers, black leather shoes, minimal black watch, and the same VANTA Carry-On 01 suitcase with matte graphite hard shell, subtle horizontal ribbing, black telescopic handle, four spinner wheels, discreet hardware, and understated VANTA branding.

Do not redesign or reinterpret the traveler or the suitcase.

Create a third campaign scene that feels like the journey has reached a quiet moment of arrival.

Do not create another full-body walking image.

Instead, create a closer cinematic composition focused on the traveler’s lower torso, hand, and luggage interaction. Frame the image approximately from the chest or waist down to the suitcase and shoes, or from the hand and telescopic handle down to the luggage, with a refined editorial crop.

Show the traveler standing or slowing near an arrival context such as a terminal exit, curbside pickup zone, hotel entrance, or transport drop-off point. The suitcase should be close to the body, upright, and naturally handled.

Focus on:
- the traveler’s hand on the telescopic handle
- the premium hard-shell luggage surface
- wheel detail
- shoe and floor reflections
- the coat hem and tailored trousers
- subtle cinematic environmental cues of arrival

Use the established VANTA visual world: deep navy, petrol blue, charcoal, cool steel, reflective dark flooring or wet pavement, soft amber practical lights, glass architecture, and quiet nighttime atmosphere.

Use shallow depth of field and cinematic lighting to make the frame feel intimate, premium, and resolved. The image should feel like a quiet final moment at the end of a journey.

The scene should communicate arrival, control, and calm confidence — like the final frame of a premium travel campaign.

Prioritize:
- natural hand anatomy
- realistic telescopic-handle grip
- believable luggage scale and wheel geometry
- tactile hard-shell material detail
- refined crop
- cinematic atmosphere
- clear narrative contrast from the first two frames

Avoid:
- full-body walking composition
- front-facing hero pose
- repeated in-transit movement
- crowded scenes
- bright daylight
- excessive signage
- excessive neon
- warped luggage
- distorted hands
- broken wheels
- text overlays
- watermarks

Vertical cinematic advertising composition.
```

## Production Note

The prompt sequence moved from broad continuity instructions to explicit camera-language control after the first In Transit attempt repeated too much of the Departure Hero composition.

The revised In Transit prompt therefore specifies side-profile movement, left-to-right direction, camera position, suitcase placement, negative space, and tracking-shot language. The Arrival prompt then deliberately moves away from full-body travel imagery into a closer detail narrative.

The intended progression is:

**Frontal hero → lateral motion → intimate arrival detail**

Portfolio-facing copy should describe the final campaign in terms of **recognizable traveler continuity**, **recognizable luggage continuity**, a **consistent visual world**, and a **coordinated narrative campaign**, rather than claiming exact or pixel-perfect reproduction.
