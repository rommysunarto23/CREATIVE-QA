# VANTA — AFTER HOURS

**Spec / simulated client project · Premium travel / luggage campaign**

VANTA is a fictional premium luggage brand. **AFTER HOURS** follows one traveler and one carry-on through a continuous late-night journey to demonstrate narrative sequencing, traveler/product continuity, cinematic lighting, and camera-language control.

## Final Campaign

1. **Departure Hero** — introduces traveler, luggage, nighttime world, and mood
2. **In Transit Motion** — advances the story through movement and active product use
3. **Arrival Detail Narrative** — resolves the sequence through closer human-product interaction

## QA Focus

Traveler identity · wardrobe continuity · luggage geometry · hand/handle interaction · wheel/perspective realism · lighting continuity · narrative separation.

## Skills Demonstrated

Cinematic art direction · narrative sequencing · character continuity · product continuity · camera-language control · prompt iteration · visual QA · rejection decisions.

## Disclosure

VANTA and AFTER HOURS are fictional. This was not commissioned or paid client work and no real-world commercial results are claimed.
