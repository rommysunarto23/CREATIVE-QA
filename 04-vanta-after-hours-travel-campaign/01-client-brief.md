# Client Brief — VANTA / AFTER HOURS

## Project Type

Spec / Simulated Client Project

## Brand

**VANTA** — a fictional premium luggage brand created for portfolio demonstration.

## Product

**VANTA Carry-On 01**

A premium compact hard-shell cabin suitcase with:

- Matte graphite shell
- Subtle horizontal ribbing
- Rounded rectangular proportions
- Black telescopic handle
- Four minimal spinner wheels
- Discreet brushed-metal hardware
- Small understated VANTA wordmark

## Campaign

**AFTER HOURS**

## Objective

Create a three-image cinematic travel campaign that follows one traveler and one carry-on through a continuous late-night journey.

The project should demonstrate:

- Narrative sequencing
- Recognizable traveler continuity
- Recognizable luggage continuity
- Product integration within a cinematic environment
- Realistic human-product interaction
- Camera-language variation
- Cinematic nighttime lighting
- Iterative refinement
- Commercial visual selection and QA
- Distinct narrative and marketing roles across the final assets

## Target Audience

Urban professionals and frequent travelers who respond to understated premium design, modern travel imagery, and cinematic brand storytelling.

This audience definition is part of the fictional creative brief and is not based on real customer data.

## Brand Personality

- Quiet confidence
- Premium
- Architectural
- Metropolitan
- Minimal
- Purposeful
- Cinematic
- Emotionally restrained

## Visual World

### Palette

- Deep navy
- Petrol blue
- Charcoal
- Cool steel
- Wet asphalt grey
- Muted amber
- Soft tungsten highlights

### Environment

- Modern airport or transport terminal
- Glass architecture
- Reflective flooring
- Wet pavement
- Distant aircraft / terminal lights
- Restrained signage
- Curbside or arrival-zone cues
- Modern hotel or transport entrance

### Lighting

Use cool ambient blue light balanced with small warm tungsten or amber practical lights. Maintain realistic reflections, environmental depth, and cinematic contrast.

Avoid bright daylight, beige luxury interiors, excessive neon, cyberpunk styling, and fashion-studio presentation.

## Traveler Anchor

A male traveler in his early thirties with:

- Short dark hair
- Understated, refined appearance
- Charcoal wool coat
- Black knit top
- Dark tailored trousers
- Clean black leather shoes
- Minimal black watch
- No visible luxury-logo accessories

The traveler should feel like a real premium traveler rather than a runway model.

## Product Anchor

Maintain a recognizable **VANTA Carry-On 01** design across the campaign:

- Matte graphite hard shell
- Subtle horizontal ribbing
- Rounded rectangular proportions
- Black telescopic handle
- Four minimal spinner wheels
- Discreet brushed-metal hardware
- Small understated VANTA wordmark

The product should remain commercially visible within the story without turning every frame into a static catalog composition.

## Deliverables

### 01 — Departure / Hero

A cinematic key visual introducing the traveler, suitcase, and nocturnal travel world.

**Primary role:** establish traveler, product, setting, and campaign mood.

### 02 — In Transit / Motion

A side-profile or lateral-motion scene showing the traveler actively moving through the terminal with the suitcase trailing behind.

**Primary role:** communicate journey progression, movement, and active product use.

### 03 — Arrival / Detail Narrative

A closer composition focused on the traveler's hand, telescopic handle, lower body, suitcase, and arrival environment.

**Primary role:** resolve the journey through a quieter, more intimate product interaction.

## Narrative Sequence

The campaign should read as one continuous progression:

```text
Departure
  ↓
Introduce traveler + product + nighttime world

In Transit
  ↓
Advance the journey through active movement

Arrival
  ↓
Resolve through intimate product interaction
```

The camera language should evolve with that sequence:

**Frontal hero → lateral motion → intimate arrival detail**

## Consistency Requirements

Maintain recognizable continuity in:

- Traveler identity
- Hair
- Charcoal coat
- Black knit top
- Dark trousers
- Black shoes
- Minimal watch
- Suitcase shell shape
- Horizontal ribbing
- Telescopic handle
- Wheel system
- Matte graphite finish
- Understated VANTA branding
- Nocturnal visual world

The goal is recognizable visual continuity, not a claim of pixel-perfect duplication.

## Rejection Criteria

Reject or revise images with:

- Broken hand anatomy
- Warped suitcase proportions
- Broken or missing wheels
- Implausible telescopic-handle geometry
- Incorrect luggage scale
- Fashion-runway posing
- Repeated camera composition that fails to advance the narrative
- Bright daylight
- Excessive neon
- Crowded scenes that bury the product
- Dominant unrelated signage
- Obvious AI artifacts
- Watermarks or added text overlays

An image may also be rejected even if visually attractive when its camera angle, pose, product placement, or framing fails to perform the intended narrative role.

## Success Criteria

The final set should read as one coordinated nocturnal journey:

**Departure → In Transit → Arrival**

Each image should perform a distinct narrative and commercial role while maintaining recognizable traveler continuity, recognizable luggage continuity, and a consistent visual world.

The final campaign should feel like **one traveler + one carry-on + one nocturnal journey**, not three unrelated luggage images.

## Disclosure

VANTA is a fictional brand created for portfolio demonstration. This is a spec / simulated client project and was not commissioned or paid client work.
