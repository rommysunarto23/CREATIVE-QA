# Marketing Campaign — VANTA / AFTER HOURS

## Campaign Status

**Spec / Simulated Client Campaign**

VANTA is a fictional premium luggage brand created for portfolio demonstration. The strategy below presents proposed marketing applications for the final campaign assets.

It does not describe real media placement, client approval, retail activity, customer response, sales results, or measured campaign performance.

## Campaign Name

**AFTER HOURS**

## Product

**VANTA Carry-On 01**

## Campaign Objective

Position VANTA visually as an understated premium travel brand through a coordinated nocturnal journey built around one traveler and one carry-on.

The campaign is structured as a narrative rather than a set of independent product images:

```text
Departure / Hero
Introduce traveler + product + nighttime world
        ↓
In Transit / Motion
Advance the journey through movement
        ↓
Arrival / Detail Narrative
Resolve through quieter human-product interaction
```

The three assets therefore operate as one system:

**one traveler + one carry-on + one nocturnal journey**

## Core Audience

Urban professionals and frequent travelers who respond to understated premium design, modern travel environments, and cinematic brand storytelling.

This is a fictional targeting direction used to guide the creative strategy. It is not based on real VANTA customer research or campaign data.

## Campaign Positioning

**AFTER HOURS** treats travel as a sequence of controlled, transitional moments rather than spectacle.

The campaign's visual identity is built around:

- Deep navy and petrol-blue ambient tones
- Charcoal wardrobe and graphite luggage
- Cool steel and glass architecture
- Wet or reflective surfaces
- Restrained amber practical lights
- Quiet metropolitan environments
- Purposeful movement
- Controlled, cinematic framing

The intention is to create a visual world that feels calm, modern, nocturnal, and product-aware without becoming a catalog shoot or fashion editorial.

## Narrative Strategy

The campaign moves through three different levels of narrative distance.

### 1. Departure — Establish

The opening frame introduces the traveler, product, setting, and mood.

The audience sees enough of the traveler and environment to understand the campaign world while the luggage remains clearly visible.

### 2. In Transit — Progress

The second frame changes both camera geometry and body movement.

Lateral motion communicates that the journey is actively progressing rather than simply presenting a second portrait in another part of the terminal.

### 3. Arrival — Resolve

The final frame moves closer.

By narrowing the composition to the hand, telescopic handle, luggage, lower body, reflections, and arrival context, the campaign closes with a quieter human-product moment.

This creates a deliberate visual progression:

**Frontal hero → lateral motion → intimate arrival detail**

## Messaging Hierarchy

### 1. Campaign

**AFTER HOURS**

The campaign title establishes the nocturnal concept and provides the unifying identity across all three frames.

### 2. Product

**VANTA Carry-On 01**

The product name should remain clear without adding unsupported technical specifications or performance claims.

### 3. Narrative

**Departure → In Transit → Arrival**

The sequence is the central campaign device. Each asset should reinforce a different chapter rather than functioning as an interchangeable luggage image.

### 4. Supporting Message

Supporting copy should remain concise, cinematic, and grounded in the journey shown in the imagery.

## Asset 01 — Departure / Hero

**Asset:** `assets/final/01-departure-hero-anchor.png`

### Primary Role

**Introduce traveler, product, nighttime world, and campaign mood.**

### Communication Priority

1. VANTA Carry-On 01
2. Traveler
3. Nocturnal travel setting
4. Navy / charcoal / amber campaign palette
5. Quiet cinematic tone

### Creative Rationale

The Departure Hero is the campaign's establishing frame.

Its relatively frontal composition allows the traveler and luggage to be understood together before the sequence becomes more kinetic. Reflective surfaces, airport architecture, distant lights, and warm practical accents establish the nocturnal world that carries through the remaining assets.

Because this frame performs the introduction, it is the strongest candidate for campaign applications where immediate orientation matters.

## Asset 02 — In Transit / Motion

**Asset:** `assets/final/02-in-transit-motion.png`

### Primary Role

**Show active travel progression, movement, and product use.**

### Communication Priority

1. Directional movement
2. Recognizable traveler and luggage
3. Natural suitcase interaction
4. Environmental depth
5. Continuity with the established nighttime world

### Creative Rationale

The In Transit frame is the campaign's movement chapter.

Its side-profile composition, left-to-right direction, trailing suitcase, and wider environmental space create a clear visual change from the hero.

That difference is strategically important. A first version of the scene was rejected because it repeated too much of the Departure composition. The selected image instead advances the story through deliberate camera-language correction.

This makes the asset particularly useful where the campaign needs to communicate progression rather than introduction.

### Proposed Applications

- Vertical social creative
- Carousel or story middle frame
- Secondary campaign visual
- Website narrative section
- Motion-reference concept for future creative development
- Campaign transition frame

### Suggested Copy

**Headline:**
**MOVE THROUGH THE NIGHT.**

**Supporting line:**
**The second chapter of one continuous VANTA journey.**

## Asset 03 — Arrival / Detail Narrative

**Asset:** `assets/final/03-arrival-detail-narrative.png`

### Primary Role

**Resolve the journey through closer human-product interaction.**

### Communication Priority

1. Hand and telescopic-handle interaction
2. VANTA Carry-On 01
3. Product and material detail
4. Arrival context
5. Quiet narrative resolution

### Creative Rationale

The Arrival frame intentionally slows the visual rhythm.

Rather than repeating the full-body movement of the first two scenes, the composition moves closer to the traveler's hand, luggage, shoes, reflections, and surrounding arrival cues.

The shift in scale gives the campaign a more intimate final chapter while keeping the product central to the frame.

Slightly stronger warm practical lighting helps suggest an endpoint while remaining within the established navy / charcoal / amber visual system.

### Proposed Applications

- Website secondary or closing section
- Social-story closing frame
- Product-focused campaign post
- Detail-led digital creative
- Campaign ending visual
- Narrative carousel closing frame

### Suggested Copy

**Headline:**
**ARRIVAL, AFTER HOURS.**

**Supporting line:**
**A quieter final frame in the same nocturnal journey.**

## Proposed Launch Sequence

### Phase 1 — Departure

Lead with the **Departure Hero** to establish VANTA, the traveler, the carry-on, and the nighttime visual system.

### Phase 2 — In Transit

Introduce the **In Transit Motion** frame to change the campaign's pace and show the journey actively progressing.

### Phase 3 — Arrival

Close with the **Arrival Detail Narrative** to move from broad environmental storytelling into a quieter product-and-human moment.

The launch sequence mirrors the narrative sequence rather than treating all three images as interchangeable executions.

## Creative Consistency Across Applications

To preserve the campaign identity across proposed channels:

- Keep deep navy, petrol blue, charcoal, cool steel, and restrained amber as the primary visual world
- Maintain recognizable traveler styling
- Maintain recognizable VANTA Carry-On 01 geometry and finish
- Keep the luggage commercially visible within each scene
- Use reflective surfaces and controlled nighttime lighting consistently
- Preserve a quiet, metropolitan tone
- Avoid excessive neon, bright daylight, and fashion-runway styling
- Avoid unrelated signage or graphics that compete with the product
- Preserve the role distinction between establishing hero, lateral motion, and intimate arrival detail

## Portfolio Rationale

The campaign is intended to demonstrate more than the generation of three visually related images.

It documents a broader creative workflow:

**brief → visual world → hero anchor → continuity constraints → rejected variation → camera-language correction → narrative progression → visual QA → final selection → proposed campaign application**

The rejected first In Transit image is an important part of that story. It demonstrates that selection was based not only on whether an image looked polished, but also on whether it fulfilled its role in the sequence.

The final set therefore demonstrates AI-assisted cinematic commercial storytelling through:

- Narrative sequencing
- Product integration
- Recognizable traveler continuity
- Recognizable luggage continuity
- Camera-language control
- Iterative refinement
- Visual QA
- Asset selection
- Campaign strategy
