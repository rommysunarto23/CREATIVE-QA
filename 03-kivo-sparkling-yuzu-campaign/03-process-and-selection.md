# Process & Selection Notes — KIVO / BRIGHT HIT

## Goal

Build a three-image beverage campaign in which each asset serves a different commercial role while retaining a recognizable KIVO packaging identity and a consistent cobalt-blue / citrus-yellow visual world.

The selection process prioritized brief adherence, product visibility, recognizable packaging continuity, visual differentiation, and commercial usefulness rather than visual appeal alone.

## Stage 1 — Hero Packshot Anchor

**Selected:** `assets/final/01-hero-packshot-anchor.png`

### Why It Was Selected

- Clear and dominant product presence
- Strong cobalt-blue and citrus-yellow visual identity
- Recognizable vertical KIVO wordmark and yellow stripe
- Believable chilled-product cue through condensation
- Strong graphic studio composition
- Clear visual separation from AURELIS and SOLVANE

### Campaign Role

The hero packshot establishes the product before more complex visual treatments are introduced. Its primary function is packaging recognition: the can, color system, typography hierarchy, condensation, and citrus cues are presented with minimal narrative distraction.

This image became the packaging and visual anchor for the remaining campaign assets.

### Citrus Accuracy Note

The citrus styling reads broadly as fresh yellow citrus. For a real commercial yuzu launch, botanical accuracy would require stricter review. For this fictional portfolio campaign, the selected image communicates the intended citrus-refreshment category while remaining clearly identified as a spec concept.

## Stage 2 — Dynamic Splash

**Selected:** `assets/final/02-dynamic-splash.png`

### Why It Was Selected

- Preserves recognizable KIVO packaging cues within a more complex action composition
- Introduces energetic water movement and a stronger refreshment signal
- Keeps the can readable despite splash, ice, fruit, and motion
- Adds a distinct commercial function instead of repeating the static hero shot
- Maintains the cobalt-blue / citrus-yellow campaign palette

### Campaign Role

The splash image shifts the campaign from product introduction to attention and movement. It uses water, ice, citrus, condensation, and a dynamic can angle to create a more energetic visual while keeping the product identifiable.

This makes the asset better suited conceptually to placements where immediate visual impact matters more than the restrained packaging clarity of the hero frame.

### QA Note

The composition is intentionally denser than the hero image. That density is appropriate to its role because the asset is designed around impact, movement, and refreshment rather than packaging simplicity.

The selection still required the can to remain visually dominant and recognizable within the action.

## Stage 3 — Lifestyle Hand Shot

**Selected:** `assets/final/03-lifestyle-hand-shot.png`

### Why It Was Selected

- Adds human interaction without shifting into fashion-editorial imagery
- Hand anatomy and grip read as sufficiently believable for the portfolio use case
- Can scale remains plausible
- Product remains the visual hero
- Bright urban setting adds social and aspirational context
- Condensation continues the chilled-product cue
- Background separation keeps attention on the can

### Campaign Role

The lifestyle image introduces the product into a human context after the first two assets have established the packaging and campaign energy.

The hand, outdoor urban setting, daylight, and shallow background separation make the product feel situated within a contemporary social moment while preserving strong can visibility.

Its purpose is not to explain the packaging in detail or maximize splash impact; it expands the campaign into product-in-use and lifestyle territory.

## Final Campaign Logic

```text
Hero Packshot
  ↓
Establish packaging + campaign identity

Dynamic Splash
  ↓
Add energy + refreshment + motion

Lifestyle Hand Shot
  ↓
Show product-in-use + human context
```

## Visual QA Framework

The final selection was evaluated against five practical criteria:

1. **Product recognition** — Does the KIVO can remain clearly identifiable?
2. **Packaging continuity** — Are the primary blue, yellow, white, and silver packaging cues preserved recognizably?
3. **Scene credibility** — Do the liquid, condensation, ice, hand anatomy, scale, and materials read believably enough for the intended portfolio use?
4. **Campaign differentiation** — Does each asset perform a distinct visual and marketing function?
5. **Art-direction consistency** — Do all three images remain within the same bold, graphic, beverage-focused visual world?

## Final QA Result

The selected three-image set demonstrates three distinct advertising contexts while maintaining a recognizable KIVO visual identity.

As a portfolio case study, the campaign demonstrates:

- Product art direction
- Recognizable packaging continuity
- Beverage styling
- Chilled-product cues
- Motion composition
- Human-product interaction
- Visual QA and asset selection
- Campaign-level differentiation between asset roles

The result should be understood as a fictional campaign concept and portfolio demonstration rather than evidence of real client approval or commercial performance.

## Lessons Carried Forward

- Lock the hero packaging before introducing motion or human interaction.
- Give each asset a distinct marketing function.
- Evaluate packaging continuity under increasingly complex scenes.
- Use visual QA to judge anatomy, materials, product scale, typography, and scene credibility.
- Do not confuse visual attractiveness with adherence to the brief.
- Avoid making stronger claims about exact product fidelity than the generated assets objectively support.
