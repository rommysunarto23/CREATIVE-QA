# KIVO — BRIGHT HIT

**Spec / simulated client project · Beverage / commercial product advertising**

KIVO is a fictional sparkling-water brand. **BRIGHT HIT** explores packaging fidelity across a hero packshot, high-energy splash composition, and lifestyle product-in-use scene.

## Final Campaign

1. **Hero Packshot** — brand and packaging introduction
2. **Dynamic Splash** — motion, refreshment, and visual impact
3. **Lifestyle Hand Shot** — product-in-use and social context

## QA Focus

Packaging proportions · brand colors · wordmark consistency · condensation/material realism · splash artifacts · hand anatomy · product hierarchy.

## Skills Demonstrated

Beverage art direction · packaging continuity · product realism · motion composition · human-product interaction · visual QA · campaign-system design.
