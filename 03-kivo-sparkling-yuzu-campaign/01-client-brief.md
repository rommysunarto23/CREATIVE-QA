# Client Brief — KIVO / BRIGHT HIT

## Project Type

Spec / Simulated Client Project

## Brand

**KIVO** — a fictional contemporary sparkling-water brand created for portfolio practice.

## Product

**KIVO Yuzu Sparkling Water**

## Campaign

**BRIGHT HIT**

## Objective

Create a three-image commercial beverage campaign that presents KIVO as a modern, energetic, premium-feeling sparkling-water brand while maintaining a recognizable packaging identity across multiple advertising contexts.

The project should demonstrate:

- Packaging continuity across multiple generated images
- Beverage product art direction
- Condensation and chilled-product realism
- Motion and splash composition
- Human-product interaction
- Bold color control
- Commercial visual selection and QA
- Distinct asset roles within one coordinated campaign

## Target Audience

Urban consumers approximately 20–35 who respond to modern, graphic, visually energetic beverage branding and contemporary lifestyle advertising.

## Brand Personality

- Fresh
- Sharp
- Energetic
- Modern
- Graphic
- Youthful
- Premium-feeling
- Clean and restrained rather than cluttered

## Product / Packaging Anchor

KIVO Yuzu Sparkling Water is presented in a slim 330 ml aluminum can with:

- Saturated cobalt-blue matte body
- Clean white vertical **KIVO** wordmark
- One bold citrus-yellow vertical accent stripe
- Small minimal **YUZU SPARKLING WATER** text
- Brushed-silver aluminum top
- No illustrations on the can
- No excessive decorative graphics

The generated campaign should preserve a recognizable version of this packaging identity rather than redesigning the can between scenes.

## Visual Direction

### Core Palette

- Saturated cobalt blue
- Vivid citrus yellow
- Crisp white
- Cool metallic silver
- Small natural green accents

### Material / Surface Language

- Matte aluminum
- Cold condensation droplets
- Clear sparkling water
- Ice
- Glossy studio surfaces
- Fresh citrus texture

### Lighting

Use sharp, energetic commercial lighting with strong contrast, crisp highlights, and controlled reflections.

Avoid the warm luxury-beauty world of AURELIS and the cool architectural-fashion world of SOLVANE.

## Deliverables

### 01 — Hero Packshot

A static commercial hero image that establishes the packaging, campaign palette, chilled-product cue, and overall KIVO visual identity.

**Primary role:** brand and packaging introduction.

### 02 — Dynamic Splash

A high-energy action image using water, ice, citrus, and motion while maintaining clear product recognition.

**Primary role:** attention, refreshment, and campaign energy.

### 03 — Lifestyle Hand Shot

A product-in-use image showing a realistic human hand holding the chilled can in a bright, contemporary urban setting.

**Primary role:** human interaction, social context, and lifestyle application.

## Visual Separation From Previous Portfolio Projects

- **AURELIS:** warm, tactile, luxury beauty/product advertising
- **SOLVANE:** cool, architectural, fashion/editorial imagery
- **KIVO:** bold, graphic, energetic beverage advertising

## Consistency Requirements

Maintain recognizable continuity in:

- Can proportions and overall geometry
- Cobalt-blue matte body
- White vertical **KIVO** wordmark
- Citrus-yellow vertical stripe
- Minimal product descriptor
- Brushed-silver aluminum top
- Overall packaging hierarchy
- Cobalt-blue / citrus-yellow campaign palette

The goal is recognizable visual continuity across generated scenes rather than a claim of pixel-perfect packaging reproduction.

## Rejection Criteria

Reject or revise images with:

- Warped or implausible can geometry
- Major packaging redesign between scenes
- Unreadable or badly distorted core wordmark
- Unrealistic condensation or liquid behavior
- Distorted citrus or ice
- Broken hand anatomy in the lifestyle image
- Excessive props that overwhelm the product
- Warm beige luxury styling that overlaps with AURELIS
- Fashion-editorial styling that overlaps with SOLVANE
- Unrelated branding, watermarks, or large added promotional text

## Success Criteria

The final campaign should read as three coordinated commercial assets for one beverage launch concept while giving each image a distinct marketing role: packaging clarity, energetic refreshment, and product-in-use lifestyle context.

Individually, each image should serve a clear advertising purpose. Collectively, the set should demonstrate recognizable KIVO packaging continuity, consistent art direction, and intentional campaign structure.

## Disclosure

KIVO is a fictional brand created for portfolio demonstration. This project was not commissioned client work.
