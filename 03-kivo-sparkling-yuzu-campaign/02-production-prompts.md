# Production Prompts — KIVO / BRIGHT HIT

These are the production prompts used to create the final three-image KIVO campaign. Repetition is intentional where it reinforces packaging continuity, visual consistency, and art direction.

## Prompt 01 — Hero Packshot

```text
Create a bold premium beverage advertising image for a fictional brand called KIVO.

The product is KIVO Yuzu Sparkling Water in a slim 330 ml aluminum can.

The can has a saturated cobalt-blue matte body, a clean white vertical KIVO wordmark, one bold citrus-yellow vertical accent stripe, small minimal text reading “YUZU SPARKLING WATER,” and a brushed-silver aluminum top.

Keep the packaging contemporary, graphic, minimal, premium, and believable. Do not add illustrations or excessive decorative graphics to the can.

Create a high-impact commercial hero packshot with the can standing upright as the dominant subject.

Build a visual world using saturated cobalt blue, vivid citrus yellow, crisp white, cool metallic silver, and small touches of fresh natural green from real yuzu leaves.

Use a bold modern studio set with clean geometric surfaces, glossy acrylic or lacquered materials, sharp graphic shadows, controlled reflections, and energetic commercial lighting.

Include one or two fresh sliced yuzu fruits around the set as restrained supporting elements, with realistic juicy pulp and natural texture.

Add cold condensation droplets to the can so it feels freshly chilled.

The image should feel crisp, refreshing, graphic, youthful, and premium — like a contemporary international beverage advertising campaign.

Use sharper, more energetic lighting than a luxury beauty campaign. The composition should feel visually bold rather than soft or neutral.

Prioritize:
- accurate cylindrical can geometry
- realistic aluminum material
- believable condensation
- readable clean packaging
- realistic yuzu fruit texture
- strong commercial composition

Avoid beige or stone luxury environments, fashion-editorial architecture, warm golden beauty lighting, excessive props, messy typography, warped cans, distorted fruit, duplicate objects, watermarks, and unrelated branding.

No additional advertising copy outside the packaging.

Vertical commercial advertising composition.
```

## Prompt 02 — Dynamic Splash

```text
Create a dynamic premium beverage advertising image for KIVO using the provided hero packshot as the exact packaging reference.

Preserve the same can design exactly: a slim 330 ml aluminum can with a saturated cobalt-blue matte body, a clean white vertical KIVO wordmark, one bold citrus-yellow vertical accent stripe, small minimal “YUZU SPARKLING WATER” text, and a brushed-silver aluminum top.

Do not redesign, simplify, or reinterpret the can graphics.

Create a high-energy commercial splash composition with the can as the hero subject. Show the can slightly tilted or dynamically positioned, surrounded by a bold, refreshing burst of sparkling water splash and droplets. Include one or two realistic yuzu fruits or yuzu slices in motion as supporting elements, with natural textured citrus skin and juicy pulp.

Keep the visual world consistent with the KIVO campaign: saturated cobalt blue, vivid citrus yellow, crisp white, cool metallic silver, and small natural green accents from real leaves. Use sharp, energetic studio lighting, glossy reflections, and strong commercial contrast.

The image should feel fresh, crisp, cold, youthful, and premium — like a modern international sparkling-water advertising campaign. Emphasize motion, refreshment, and impact while keeping the packaging clearly readable.

Prioritize:
- exact can identity consistency
- realistic water splash physics
- believable condensation
- readable clean packaging
- realistic citrus texture
- premium commercial composition

Avoid beige luxury styling, fashion-editorial environments, overly soft lighting, too many fruits, messy clutter, warped cans, distorted liquid, text outside the packaging, watermarks, and unrelated branding.

Vertical commercial advertising composition.
```

## Prompt 03 — Lifestyle Hand Shot

```text
Create a premium lifestyle beverage advertising image for KIVO using the provided hero packshot as the exact packaging reference.

Preserve the same can design exactly: a slim 330 ml aluminum can with a saturated cobalt-blue matte body, a clean white vertical KIVO wordmark, one bold citrus-yellow vertical accent stripe, small minimal “YUZU SPARKLING WATER” text, and a brushed-silver aluminum top.

Do not redesign, simplify, or reinterpret the can graphics.

Create a product-in-use lifestyle composition showing a human hand naturally holding the chilled KIVO can in a bright modern urban setting, such as a stylish café terrace, rooftop, or clean contemporary outdoor environment.

The hand should feel natural, realistic, and commercially polished. The can must remain the visual hero.

Show subtle cold condensation on the can, with a fresh premium lifestyle feel. You may include restrained supporting citrus cues such as one yuzu slice or a small hint of green leaves nearby, but keep the styling minimal and believable.

Use bright natural daylight with a clean, contemporary, youthful atmosphere. The background should be softly blurred and modern, suggesting an aspirational lifestyle without becoming busy or distracting.

The image should feel refreshing, social, modern, premium, and commercially attractive — like a real international sparkling-water lifestyle campaign.

Prioritize:
- exact packaging consistency
- realistic human hand anatomy
- believable can scale
- natural grip
- realistic condensation
- clean product visibility
- premium commercial lifestyle composition

Avoid fashion-editorial posing, full-body humans, cluttered café tables, messy props, warped hands, distorted fingers, excessive fruit, overly busy backgrounds, watermarks, and text outside the packaging.

Vertical commercial lifestyle composition.
```

## Production Note

The prompts intentionally use strong packaging-reference language to reinforce continuity during generation. In portfolio documentation, the final result should be described as demonstrating **recognizable packaging continuity**, not pixel-perfect or exact duplication unless the generated assets objectively support that stronger claim.
