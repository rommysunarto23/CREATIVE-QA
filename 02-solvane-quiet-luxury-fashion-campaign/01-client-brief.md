# Client Brief — SOLVANE / Quiet Movement

## Project Type

**Spec / Simulated Client Project**

## Client Background

SOLVANE is a fictional contemporary luxury womenswear label built around quiet luxury: modern tailoring, restrained confidence, architectural minimalism, and material quality rather than visible logos or flamboyant styling.

## Campaign Title

**QUIET MOVEMENT — Autumn Editorial Campaign**

## Objective

Create a three-image digital fashion campaign that feels like one coordinated editorial photoshoot while demonstrating recognizable continuity across model identity, wardrobe, accessories, lighting, and art direction.

The campaign should demonstrate:

- Realistic human anatomy and natural skin
- Recognizable model continuity across the series
- Consistent garments and accessories
- Believable tailoring and fabric behavior
- Controlled cool-neutral color direction
- Distinct compositions within one visual universe

## Target Audience

Women approximately 25–40, especially urban professionals and creative-industry consumers who prefer understated premium fashion.

## Brand Personality

- Quiet confidence
- Architectural minimalism
- Modern tailoring
- Understated elegance
- Urban sophistication
- Editorial restraint
- Material quality
- Effortless luxury

## Visual Universe

### Environment Palette

- Slate grey
- Soft concrete grey
- Muted charcoal
- Smoked taupe
- Cool off-white
- Steel grey

### Wardrobe Contrast

- Camel beige
- Warm sand
- Ivory silk
- Dark espresso brown
- Subtle gold

### Environment Materials

- Smooth architectural concrete
- Smoked glass
- Muted grey stone
- Brushed steel / aluminum
- Dark metal framing
- Linear contemporary architecture

### Lighting

Use soft neutral daylight or slightly overcast editorial daylight with controlled directional shadows. Avoid dominant amber or golden beauty lighting.

## Hero Model Anchor

A sophisticated female fashion model in her late twenties with shoulder-length dark brown hair, refined natural makeup, a warm-neutral complexion, natural skin texture, and a calm, self-assured editorial expression.

## Wardrobe Anchor

- Oversized camel-beige tailored blazer
- Matching high-waisted wide-leg trousers
- Ivory silk camisole
- Minimalist gold earrings
- Structured dark-brown leather handbag

The wardrobe and accessories should remain recognizable and visually consistent across all three images.

## Deliverables

### 01 — Editorial Hero

Full-body or near-full-body campaign key visual in a cool contemporary architectural environment.

### 02 — Urban Movement

Full-body walking image showing natural garment movement and confident metropolitan energy.

### 03 — Tailoring Detail

Close editorial crop emphasizing lapel construction, fabric weave, silk, leather grain, hand gesture, and material craftsmanship.

## Consistency Requirements

Aim to maintain recognizable continuity in:

- Face and hair
- Makeup and skin appearance
- Blazer color and construction
- Trouser silhouette
- Camisole
- Gold earrings
- Handbag shape and color
- Overall styling

## Rejection Criteria

Reject images with:

- Extra, missing, or distorted fingers
- Merged hands or handbag handles
- Warped garment seams
- Material changes to lapels, buttons, or accessories
- Impossible folds or body proportions
- Plastic-looking skin
- Warm spa or beauty styling that overlaps with AURELIS
- Obvious AI artifacts

## Success Criteria

Individually, each image should work as a luxury fashion editorial image. Collectively, the final set should read as one coordinated campaign with a recognizable model, wardrobe, collection, and visual world.
