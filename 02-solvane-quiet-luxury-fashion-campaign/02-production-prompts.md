# Production Prompts

These prompts were used to develop the final SOLVANE campaign. Repetition is retained where it supports model, wardrobe, environment, anatomy, and material continuity.

## Prompt 01 — Editorial Hero (Final Direction)

```text
Create a premium fashion editorial campaign image for a fictional contemporary luxury fashion brand called SOLVANE.

Feature a sophisticated female fashion model in her late twenties with shoulder-length dark brown hair, refined natural makeup, a warm-neutral complexion, and an elegant, understated appearance.

She wears an oversized camel-beige tailored blazer with matching high-waisted wide-leg trousers, an ivory silk camisole, minimalist gold earrings, and carries a structured dark-brown leather handbag.

The styling should represent modern quiet luxury: refined, minimal, sophisticated, expensive, and effortless.

Create a high-end, full-body editorial portrait in a cool contemporary architectural setting that feels clearly different from a warm beauty campaign. Use a minimalist modern environment with smooth concrete, smoked glass, muted grey walls, subtle brushed-metal accents, clean linear forms, and restrained urban-luxury design.

Use soft neutral daylight or slightly overcast editorial lighting with gentle directional shadows. Keep the overall palette cooler and more restrained, with tones such as slate grey, soft concrete grey, charcoal, smoked taupe, and cool off-white, so the camel outfit stands out clearly.

The image should feel like a real international luxury fashion editorial photographed for a premium magazine—elegant, modern, urban, and visually polished.

Prioritize realistic human anatomy, natural hands, believable fabric drape, authentic tailoring, subtle movement, and sophisticated posing.

Avoid warm beige spa-like interiors, beauty-product styling, excessive golden sunlight, clutter, glossy or plastic-looking skin, distorted anatomy, extra fingers, artificial facial features, text, logos, and watermarks.

Vertical fashion editorial composition.
```

## Prompt 02 — Urban Movement

```text
Create an urban movement fashion campaign image for SOLVANE using the provided editorial hero image as the exact character, wardrobe, and styling reference.

Preserve the same model identity, facial features, shoulder-length dark brown hair, natural makeup, camel-beige oversized tailored blazer, matching high-waisted wide-leg trousers, ivory silk camisole, minimalist gold earrings, and structured dark-brown leather handbag.

Do not redesign, simplify, or reinterpret the outfit or accessories.

Show the model walking naturally through a cool contemporary urban architectural environment with smooth concrete, smoked glass, dark metal framing, muted grey stone, and restrained modern design.

The environment must remain within the same cool editorial visual world as the hero image. Avoid warm beige interiors, golden luxury lighting, spa-like architecture, or beauty-campaign styling.

Use soft neutral daylight with slightly overcast editorial lighting and subtle directional shadows. The model should remain the visual focus while the background feels metropolitan, minimal, and premium.

Capture natural walking motion: slight movement in the blazer and wide-leg trousers, realistic body balance, believable hand positioning, and natural interaction with the handbag.

The image should feel like a candid but highly art-directed luxury fashion editorial photographed on location for an international magazine.

Prioritize realistic anatomy, natural hands, believable fabric movement, consistent wardrobe construction, and authentic material texture.

Avoid crowds, excessive signage, dominant vehicles, distorted anatomy, duplicated accessories, altered wardrobe details, text, logos, and watermarks.

Vertical full-body editorial composition.
```

## Prompt 03 — Tailoring Detail

```text
Create a close-cropped luxury fashion editorial detail image for SOLVANE using the provided hero image as the exact character, wardrobe, and styling reference.

Preserve the established wardrobe exactly: the camel-beige oversized tailored blazer, matching high-waisted wide-leg trousers, ivory silk camisole, minimalist gold earrings, and structured dark-brown leather handbag.

Do not redesign, simplify, or reinterpret any garment or accessory.

Create a sophisticated editorial crop framed approximately from the lower face or shoulder area down to the upper thigh. Do not show the full body.

The visual focus should be on tailoring, material quality, and subtle human gesture rather than on the model's face.

Show one hand naturally holding the structured dark-brown leather handbag while the other hand lightly adjusts the blazer lapel, rests near the waist, or interacts naturally with the tailoring.

Highlight:
- the blazer lapel construction
- camel fabric weave and texture
- natural folds and stitching
- ivory silk camisole texture
- structured leather handbag grain
- subtle gold jewelry
- realistic hands and fingers

Use the same cool contemporary architectural world established in the campaign: muted concrete grey, smoked glass, charcoal metal framing, and soft neutral daylight.

Use a shallow depth of field and refined directional lighting so the materials feel tactile, expensive, and photographic.

The image should resemble a close fashion detail photograph from an international luxury editorial campaign, not an e-commerce catalog image or a beauty portrait.

Use an intentionally asymmetric crop and editorial framing. The image should feel intimate, tactile, restrained, and premium.

Prioritize realistic hands, authentic garment construction, believable fabric physics, natural skin texture, and material fidelity.

Avoid full-body composition, centered standing poses, warm beige interiors, golden beauty lighting, altered wardrobe details, excessive accessories, distorted fingers, impossible folds, text, logos, and watermarks.

Vertical editorial composition.
```
