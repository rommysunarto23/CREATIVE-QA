# SOLVANE — Quiet Movement

**Spec / simulated client project · Luxury fashion editorial**

SOLVANE is a fictional fashion brand used to demonstrate wardrobe continuity, realistic human rendering, camera-language variation, and editorial art direction across a coordinated campaign.

## Final Campaign

1. **Editorial Hero** — establishes model, wardrobe, palette, and visual identity
2. **Urban Movement** — adds motion and architectural context
3. **Tailoring Detail** — focuses on fabric, lapels, accessories, and hand realism

## QA Focus

Model continuity · anatomy · hands/fingers · garment construction · material texture · accessory consistency · camera-language differentiation.

## Skills Demonstrated

AI fashion art direction · character continuity · anatomy QA · material fidelity · prompt iteration · editorial composition · asset selection.
