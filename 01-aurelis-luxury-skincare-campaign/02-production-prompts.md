# Production Prompts

These prompts document the production path used to develop the final AURELIS campaign. The hero image established the visual anchor; later prompts were refined to preserve the core product identity while changing context and camera language.

---

## 1. Hero Product Shot

```text
Create a premium luxury skincare advertising image for a fictional brand called AURELIS.

The main subject is “AURELIS Radiance Renewal Serum,” a luxury skincare serum presented in a frosted warm-ivory glass bottle with a sleek cylindrical silhouette, matte midnight-blue dropper cap, minimalist elegant label, refined proportions, and a premium clean-beauty aesthetic.

Show a single hero product centered in a polished studio environment. The composition should resemble a high-end beauty campaign created for a premium skincare launch. Use soft, sculpted lighting, subtle reflections, elegant shadows, and a refined luxury atmosphere.

Place the bottle on a clean pedestal or stone surface within a warm ivory and muted beige environment. Introduce subtle midnight-blue accents and a soft touch of golden light to reinforce the campaign palette.

The image should feel minimal, luxurious, editorial, and highly polished. It should resemble a real premium commercial beauty advertisement. Keep the product design crisp, believable, and visually consistent.

Avoid clutter, excessive props, and obvious AI artifacts.

No large typography, poster-style layout, or watermark.
```

---

## 2. Lifestyle Editorial Shot — Refined

```text
Create a luxury lifestyle beauty campaign image for AURELIS using the provided hero product image as the product reference.

Preserve the established product design: a premium skincare serum bottle with a frosted warm-ivory glass body, sleek cylindrical silhouette, matte midnight-blue dropper cap, thin gold ring at the neck, and refined minimalist AURELIS label. Keep the bottle shape, proportions, color palette, and overall premium packaging consistent with the reference image.

Place the serum in an upscale editorial lifestyle setting, such as a refined modern vanity or luxury bathroom illuminated by soft natural morning light. Use elegant stone, ceramic, brushed metal, or glass accents while keeping the product as the clear visual focus.

The setting should feel warm, calm, sophisticated, and luxurious.

The composition should resemble a premium skincare campaign created for social media or beauty editorial use. Keep the image polished, believable, commercially appealing, and visually restrained.

Avoid excessive props, clutter, distracting objects, and obvious AI artifacts.

Important: maintain strong visual consistency with the reference product. Keep the label elegant and minimal, without unnecessary text or promotional slogans.

No large typography or watermark.
```

---

## 3. Macro Detail Shot — Final

```text
Create a true macro luxury beauty detail shot for AURELIS using the established product design as the reference.

Show a tightly cropped editorial close-up of the AURELIS Radiance Renewal Serum bottle. Preserve the established product identity: frosted warm-ivory glass, sleek cylindrical form, matte midnight-blue dropper cap, and thin gold ring at the neck. Keep the design visually consistent with the previous hero and lifestyle images.

Focus on craftsmanship and material quality rather than presenting the entire product in a standard centered composition. Use macro or close-cropped framing to highlight premium details such as the frosted glass texture, elegant gold neck ring, smooth curve of the bottle shoulder, refined surface of the dropper cap, and soft luxury reflections.

Use warm neutral lighting with a high-end beauty advertising aesthetic. The image should feel intimate, refined, tactile, and premium, resembling a close-up detail image from a luxury skincare campaign.

Keep the environment minimal and softly blurred. Avoid clutter, distracting props, unnecessary promotional text, and obvious AI artifacts.

Important: this image should feel clearly distinct from the hero shot, with a true macro editorial focus on materials, texture, and craftsmanship rather than a full-product packshot.

No large typography or watermark.
```

## Production Note

Exact package typography was not treated as a pixel-locked packaging reproduction task. The consistency target was the **recognizable product identity**: bottle silhouette, warm-ivory frosted glass, midnight-blue cap, gold neck ring, and overall AURELIS luxury palette.
