# Client Brief — AURELIS

## Project

**AURELIS — Premium Skincare Serum Launch Campaign**

## Project Type

**Spec / simulated client project**

AURELIS is a fictional brand created for portfolio practice. This project was not commissioned client work.

## Client Background

AURELIS is a fictional premium skincare brand preparing to launch its flagship product, **Radiance Renewal Serum**. The brand should feel modern, refined, clean, and high-end.

## Objective

Create three cohesive commercial visuals that demonstrate the ability to follow a creative brief, maintain a recognizable product identity, and adapt one visual system across multiple advertising contexts.

## Target Audience

Urban professionals aged approximately 25–40 who value premium skincare, visual sophistication, and modern clean-beauty branding.

## Product Identity

- Frosted warm-ivory glass serum bottle
- Sleek cylindrical silhouette
- Matte midnight-blue dropper cap
- Thin gold ring at the neck
- Minimal, elegant label
- Premium clean-beauty aesthetic

## Brand Direction

**Keywords:** premium, minimal luxury, modern, clean beauty, refined, editorial, elegant

**Palette:** warm ivory, soft beige, muted stone, midnight blue, subtle gold

## Deliverables

1. **Hero Product Shot** — primary launch / website hero visual
2. **Lifestyle Editorial Shot** — aspirational brand-context visual
3. **Macro Detail Shot** — material, craftsmanship, and premium-finish visual

## Intended Use

- Website hero section
- Social media
- Digital advertising
- Brand portfolio showcase

## Requirements

- Maintain a recognizable product identity across all three images
- Keep the product premium, polished, and believable
- Preserve the core bottle silhouette, cap color, gold neck ring, and overall palette
- Avoid clutter and unnecessary promotional text
- Avoid watermarks and obvious AI artifacts
- Ensure each image serves a distinct campaign purpose

## Success Criteria

The final series should feel like a cohesive luxury campaign rather than three unrelated AI-generated images. Each frame should have a distinct commercial role while remaining recognizably part of the same AURELIS visual system.
